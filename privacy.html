Fit 2 Compete privacy

Fit 2 Compete is a training and nutrition app run by Set Grow Training & Consulting for a small group of invited athletes and their families. Last updated November 26, 2026.

What we keep

Your sign-in email, the answers you give when building a plan, your workout and meal logs, body scans you upload, supplement lists, and, if you connect a wearable such as WHOOP, its daily recovery, sleep, strain, workout and body-measurement data.

Where it's kept

In a private Google Sheet and Google Drive folder owned by Set Grow Training & Consulting. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything other than coaching you.

Who can see it

You, a parent or guardian you link (for athletes under 18), and the coach. Coaching answers are prepared with Claude, an AI assistant, using only the data needed for that answer.

Wearable connections

A wearable connection only reads data; it never changes anything in your WHOOP or other account. You can disconnect at any time in the app or in your WHOOP account settings, and we stop collecting new data right away.

Deleting your data

Email setgrowtrain@gmail.com and your records are deleted within 30 days.
